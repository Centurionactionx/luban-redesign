## Platform

web

## Users

Luban primarily serves Canadian high-school students in grades 9–11, especially students in underserved communities who have limited access to university guidance, practical business education, career insight, and professional networks.

Specific programs may serve a broader audience, including students in grades 9–12 and university students. Secondary participants and supporters include educators, mentors, sponsors, admission officers, industry professionals, judges, guest speakers, and high-school students serving in youth executive roles.

## Product Purpose

Luban prepares young people for post-secondary education and careers in business. It exists to close access gaps between school and university or career opportunities through free practical learning, guidance, exposure, and mentorship.

Success means students gain useful business experience, clearer post-secondary and career direction, stronger confidence, and access to people and opportunities that would otherwise be difficult to reach.

## Positioning

Luban is a registered, youth-led nonprofit operating across multiple Canadian provinces. Its distinct model combines four active pillars in one free program ecosystem:

- hands-on business events;
- post-secondary planning and guidance;
- career exploration and pathfinding;
- mentorship and professional connection.

The combination of youth leadership, practical business learning, multiprovincial reach, and an explicit focus on underserved communities is central to the product.

## Operating Context

Students discover Luban and its programs through the public website, review event formats and requirements, and register through external services such as Google Forms or Luban's Linktree. Programs may run virtually or in person; current virtual workflows use tools such as Zoom, presentation slides, breakout rooms, email, and online registration forms.

Hands-on programming includes case competitions and interactive simulations. Participants may research and present in teams, practice negotiation and resource management, receive instruction and feedback, and connect with judges or industry professionals. Luban also recruits youth volunteers to support outreach, partnerships, event planning, and operations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- All four program pillars are active: hands-on events, post-secondary guidance, career pathfinding, and mentorship.
- Programming is free to participants and supports virtual and in-person delivery.
- The website is a static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript project deployed through Vercel.
- Event registration currently depends on external services, including Google Forms and Linktree.
- General inquiries and executive applications are delivered through EmailJS.
- Some application workflows collect personal information from minors, including names, email addresses, grade levels, schools, portfolio links, and written statements.
- Published April–June 2026 event and recruitment dates are historical and must not be presented as current without new confirmation.

## Product Principles

1. **Expand access.** Prioritize students and communities with fewer paths to business education, university guidance, mentorship, and professional networks.
2. **Learn by doing.** Turn abstract academic and career concepts into practical experiences, simulations, competitions, conversations, and guided application.
3. **Bridge school and the future.** Help students connect present choices with post-secondary education and credible career possibilities.
4. **Lead with youth, supported by expertise.** Preserve meaningful youth ownership while connecting participants with experienced educators, mentors, partners, and industry professionals.
5. **Earn trust through consistency.** Keep programs free, expectations clear, curriculum standards coherent, partnerships truthful, and organizational claims supportable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Luban's mission explicitly prioritizes underserved youth and equitable access to educational and professional opportunities. The product serves minors and should use clear, age-appropriate language and low-barrier participation paths.

A formal accessibility target and product-specific inclusion requirements have not yet been confirmed. These remain open decisions and must not be inferred from the current interface.
