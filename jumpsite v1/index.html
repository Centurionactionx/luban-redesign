Jumpstart was co-founded in 2025 to provide interactive and creative STEAM workshops that inspire young learners and ignite curiosity beyond the classroom.

We collaborate with schools and afterschool programs to deliver lessons and activities that make STEAM subjects engaging, practical, and fun for students of all ages.

Our programs are carefully designed to foster creativity, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills, helping students discover their passion for STEAM.

Jumpstart provides guidance, resources, and mentoring to ensure that educators and students can make the most of every lesson and activity.
