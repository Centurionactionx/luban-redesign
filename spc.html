About the Event

What is a Stock Pitch Competition?

A Stock Pitch Competition (SPC) is a case competition where teams analyze a publicly traded company's stock and develop a buy, sell, or hold recommendation backed by your investment thesis, research, and valuation.

This event is designed for high school students with varying levels of investing experience — you don't need to be an expert. What matters is your reasoning, your research, and your ability to communicate a clear investment case.

Grades 9–12 Teams of 5 Free to Enter Online (Zoom)

Qualitative Research

Dig into the company's business model, competitive position, and industry trends.

Financial Analysis

Review financial statements and apply basic ratios to support your thesis.

Q&A with Judges

Defend your recommendation live with 3 minutes of Q&A from industry professionals.

Teamwork

Collaborate with a team of up to 5 — solo entrants are matched into complete teams.

Important Notes

Before You Register

Team size: Teams are made up of 5 members. Individuals or incomplete teams will be matched with others. If your team has fewer than 5 members, you must email johnny.y.sun2009@gmail.com before the application deadline.

Presentation format: Your pitch must be delivered via slides on Zoom. Each presentation is 10 minutes total — 7 minutes for your pitch and 3 minutes for judge Q&A.

Valuations: Rigorous DCF or advanced valuations are not required — but judges will appreciate the effort and skill you demonstrate. Focus on a clear, well-reasoned thesis.

Attendance: You are only required to attend the opening ceremony, closing ceremony, and your own presentation timeslot. You are welcome to watch other teams — please be respectful while doing so.

Timeslot delivery: Your presentation time will be emailed to your team on the morning of April 5th. For any special scheduling requests, contact johnny.y.sun2009@gmail.com.
