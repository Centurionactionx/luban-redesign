About the Event

Play, Negotiate, and Strategize

Luban's Economics Competition places you and your teammates inside a dynamic, real-time mock-economy simulation. The team that accumulates the most value by the end of the simulation wins the cash prize!

With the support of our partners StudyScope and CFEE, this interactive event teaches fundamental economic concepts in a collaborative, fast-paced environment where you can immediately apply classroom ideas to sandbox negotiations.

High School & University Mock-Economy Game Cash Prizes No Prep Required

Resource Management

Allocate resources strategically and make critical budgeting decisions as economic indicators change.

Active Negotiation

Trade and cut deals with rival teams to secure components, raise value, and achieve mutual advantages.

Real-world Debrief

Following the 1-hour game, unpack the mechanics of inflation, supply/demand, and game theory you used.

Event Schedule

1:30 PM EST: Welcome & Onboarding

Log in, meet the coordinators, learn the simulation game mechanics, study the interface controls, and align with your team members in private breakout channels.

1:45 PM EST: Simulation Begins

The live economy simulation launches! Run trade cycles, purchase resources, negotiate with other players, and maximize your team's value over roughly 1 hour.

2:45 PM EST: Tally & Analysis

Simulation rounds end. The final values of all teams are logged, audited, and compiled. Grab a quick water break while results are verified.

3:00 PM EST: Economic Concepts Debrief

Learn how the principles of scarcity, micro-economics, and negotiation played out in the game. Real-world applications are broken down in an interactive debriefing session.

3:20 PM EST: Winners Announced & Wrap-up

The winning teams are revealed, cash prizes are distributed, and key takeaways are shared.

Important Info

Before You Play

Keep these key details in mind as you prepare for the simulation event.

Eligibility: Any high school or university student is welcome to apply. You don't need any prior business or economics coursework—the rules will be taught from scratch at the beginning of the event!

Team Dynamics: Teams will consist of 2 to 4 players. If you don't have a team, register individually and we'll pair you with teammates before kickoff so you don't miss out on the action.

Platform & Setup: You'll need a stable internet connection and a laptop or desktop computer to run the simulation interface and access the Zoom session concurrently.
